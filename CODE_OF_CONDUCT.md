# Project conduct

Standards welcomes careful disagreement about design and implementation. Critique the
work, explain your reasoning and leave room for another person's experience. Be clear
about uncertainty and give credit for others' work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualised attention, deliberate
disruption and disclosure of private information are not acceptable in project issues,
reviews or other project-managed spaces. Do not submit another person's work without the
rights and attribution it requires.

For a public incident, use GitHub's reporting tools where appropriate and ask a maintainer
to moderate the thread. For a sensitive incident, request a private conduct-report contact
route in an issue without naming people or describing the incident publicly. Maintainers
should arrange a private channel before requesting details, share only what is necessary
to handle the report, and explain any confidentiality limits. No private conduct-report
address is currently designated by this policy. Report security vulnerabilities using
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct route.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, warn participants, remove content or restrict
participation in proportion to the conduct and its impact. Explain the decision to the
affected participant where feasible without exposing the reporter or private information.
Retaliation for a good-faith report is itself a violation. Appeals follow
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#disagreement-and-appeal); a separate reviewer may not be
available. This policy does not appoint additional moderators or promise a response time.
